How to Secure Your Crypto Wallet

5 min read | By Security Expert

In crypto, you are your own bank. That means if you lose your keys, you lose your funds. Security is not optional. Here is how to keep your digital assets safe.

1. Hardware Wallets (Cold Storage)

The gold standard for security. Devices like Ledger or Trezor keep your private keys offline. Unlike "hot wallets" (MetaMask, Trust Wallet), they cannot be hacked remotely.

2. Seed Phrase Hygiene

Your 12 or 24-word seed phrase IS your money. Follow these rules:

  • NEVER type it into a computer or phone (keyloggers exist).
  • NEVER take a photo of it.
  • Write it down on paper (or steel) and store it in a fireproof safe.

3. Beware of Phishing

Scammers will create fake websites that look exactly like Uniswap or your exchange. Always double-check URLs. Bookmark your most-used sites.
